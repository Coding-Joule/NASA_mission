Last Chance Mission

The Boss

Listen up. Your last few missions didn't go well.

This is your one last chance: plan three missions, to the Moon, Mars and Europa.

Get the crew there, bring back the science, and get them home. Mess up, and you're fired.

  1. Read the briefing. Real NASA numbers tell you how hard each world is.
  2. Load the rocket. The rocket only has room for so much. Work out the fuel to land and take off, food for both ways, and whether you need a heater or a cooler.
  3. Fly and land. Run out of food and the crew starves; run out of fuel and you crash. Disasters will happen, and you only get 10 seconds to decide: fix them, or lose the mission.
  4. Collect the science. Play mini games to drill rocks, snap pictures and scoop the ground before time runs out: 3 minutes, then 2, then 1.
  5. Take off, fly home and face the boss. Get 3 of 4 right on the boss's quiz about the NASA data to keep your job.

What if…? Solve real space problems

Each one is a real problem NASA faced. Pick how you'd solve it, then see what NASA really did.
